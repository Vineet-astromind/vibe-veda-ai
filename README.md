 VibeVeda AI: The Contextual Aesthetic Engine

> **"Don't search with keywords. Search with Feelings."**

VibeVeda is an intelligent, multi-agent AI system that decodes the "Vibe" of any image. It doesn't just tell you what's in the photo—it tells you how it *feels*, what music matches it, and how to dress for it.

---

##  Why I Made This
As a 1st-semester student, I noticed a gap in how we search. We often see a cool photo (a sunset, a neon street, a wedding) and want to replicate that **"Aesthetic"** in our real lives.
* Traditional search engines fail at this. You can't search for "that melancholic rainy feeling" on a shopping site.
* **VibeVeda solves this** by using Multimodal AI to translate *pixels* into *culture* (Music, Fashion, Colors).

##  Key Features
* ** Visual Palette Generator:** Extracts and renders real hexadecimal color swatches from images.
* ** Occasion Context:** Adjusts recommendations based on where you are going (e.g., "Indian Wedding" vs "Tech Hackathon").
* ** Smart Integrations:** Generates direct search links for **Spotify** (Music) and **Google Shopping** (Fashion).
* ** Hyper-Local:** Optimized for the Indian context (Suggests Kurtis, Sarojini Market hacks, and Trending Hindi Pop).

##  Tech Stack
* **Brain:** Google Gemini 2.0 Flash (Multimodal Generative AI)
* **Frontend:** Streamlit (Python)
* **Logic:** Python (Regex, Urllib)
* **Deployment:** Streamlit Community Cloud

##  How It Works
1.  **Upload** an image (e.g., a photo of a moody coffee shop).
2.  **Select** your context (e.g., "First Date").
3.  **The AI Agents** debate and generate:
    * A poetic description of the vibe.
    * A matching song (e.g., *Prateek Kuhad* for cozy vibes).
    * A shopping list (High-end vs. Budget).
